# Claude Certified Architect Foundations — Preparation Guide

## What is this?

This document is based on real certification experience. We asked colleagues who passed the Claude Certified Architect Foundations exam to share feedback so you can be better prepared.

Updated: 23 April 2026

**Important notes:**

- These are **not** the exact questions or scenarios you will see on the exam, but they are close enough to help you grasp what to expect.
- The table below contains highlights from colleague feedback, grouped by the five domains from the official exam curriculum.
- **Category** indicates the quality/detail level of each entry:
  - **Tier 1** — Question with answer options recalled
  - **Tier 2** — Question recalled without answer options
  - **Tier 3** — Topic/direction only, but still helpful
- In the **Answers** column, options marked **(most likely)** reflect the personal judgment of the person who provided the feedback. Be mindful — this may or may not be the correct answer.
- The **Insights** column contains personal thoughts and observations from the authors. You may agree or disagree — that's expected and encouraged.
- **The goal is not to memorize these questions.** The goal is to understand the logic, build your own reasoning, and develop your own judgment. Use this as a starting point for deeper study, not as an answer key.
- If you disagree with a suggested answer or have a different perspective — great. That analytical thinking is exactly what the exam tests.
